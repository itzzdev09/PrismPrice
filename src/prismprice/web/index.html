Products to explore

Historical research example, not a current retail forecast. The fitted quantile model learns positive sales on observed active SKU-days through December 2011; missing days are not treated as zero demand, and stockout availability is unknown. Historical prices were not randomized, so the estimated relationship does not identify the causal effect of changing price. The source has no unit costs, inventory, competitor history, or customer retention outcomes. The workbench asks you to supply cost for its guardrails. Causal elasticity, CLV, and cross-SKU substitution are not connected to this serving path. Nothing is auto-published. Source and attribution: UCI Online Retail II, CC BY 4.0.

The method

From signal
to decision.

Each recommendation includes the estimates, constraints, and decision record used to produce it.

01 / ESTIMATE

Estimate demand

Model expected demand at each candidate price and keep uncertainty in view.

02 / COMPARE

Compare candidates

Score possible prices against expected value and downside risk.

03 / PROTECT

Apply policy

Exclude prices that breach margin, movement, inventory, or fairness rules.

Record the result

Keep the inputs, candidate scores, model versions, and reason codes.
