How prices are evaluated.

Each recommendation includes the estimates, constraints, and decision record used to produce it.

01

Estimate demand

Model expected demand at each candidate price and keep uncertainty visible.

02

Compare candidates

Score possible prices against expected value and downside risk.

03

Apply policy

Exclude prices that breach margin, movement, inventory, or fairness rules.

04

Record the result

Keep the inputs, model versions, candidate scores, and reason codes.
